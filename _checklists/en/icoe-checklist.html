🛡️ Family Emergency Dossier Security & Maintenance Guidelines:

• Never write raw passwords on plain paper: Do not write bank ATM PINs, netbanking passwords, or UPI PINs in plain text. Use a secure digital password manager (e.g., Bitwarden) with an emergency sheet access protocol.

• Fireproof & Waterproof Protection: Store all original identity documents, title deeds, and the emergency dossier inside a certified fire-resistant and waterproof document pouch.

• Legal Heir Certificate Awareness: Educate family that obtaining a Legal Heir Certificate from the local Revenue Tahsildar via E-Sevai is the essential first legal step to access family pension and bank claims; keep death certificates and ration cards accessible.

• Vehicle & Home Insurance Copies: Keep current vehicle insurance policies and comprehensive home insurance schedules ready for natural disaster or accident compensation claims.

• Annual Dossier Audit: Designate a fixed date each year (such as New Year's Day or birthdays) to review, add newly opened accounts, and refresh policy renewals in your dossier.
