Where should you park your emergency fund?

Keep a small amount of cash securely at home and high-liquidity funds in an accessible savings bank account for instant needs. Park the remaining buffer across multiple sweep-in or flexible Fixed Deposits (FDs) with minimal premature withdrawal penalties, or high-grade liquid mutual funds (with instant redemption facilities up to ₹50,000). A Recurring Deposit (RD) helps build discipline, but verify premature liquidation terms beforehand. Avoid counting locked-in or market-volatile assets towards your emergency fund.

Key Assumptions
  • Calculations account solely for unavoidable essential living costs (groceries, rent/EMI, utility bills, essential insurance premiums).
  • Because the primary purpose of an emergency fund is capital safety and instant liquidity, potential interest returns and inflation are excluded.
  • A 6-month buffer is recommended for salaried individuals, whereas 9–12+ months is advised for self-employed professionals, freelancers, or single-income households.
