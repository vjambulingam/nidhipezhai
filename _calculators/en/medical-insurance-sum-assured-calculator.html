Key Checks Before Buying a Policy

Always verify whether the policy has a room rent capping or sub-limit, which can trigger severe proportional deductions across your entire medical bill. Check co-pay clauses, disease waiting periods, pre-existing condition exclusions, and cashless network hospitals.

If you have senior citizen parents (age 60+), consider purchasing a separate individual or dedicated senior citizen policy for them rather than grouping them in your young family floater to prevent premium spikes and claim pool exhaustion.

How is this estimate calculated?
