Key assumptions
  • EMI is calculated using a monthly reducing balance and a constant interest rate.
  • Part-payments reduce outstanding principal and shorten the loan term while the scheduled EMI stays unchanged. The model excludes prepayment charges.
  • Actual floating rates, fees, repayment dates and lender terms can change the outcome.
