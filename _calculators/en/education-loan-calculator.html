Key Assumptions
  • Simple interest applies during the moratorium period, and compound reducing balance interest applies during the repayment phase.
  • Government interest subsidies (e.g. Central Sector Interest Subsidy - CSIS) and female student concessions may reduce net interest cost.
  • Under Section 80E of the Income Tax Act, the entire interest paid on an education loan is deductible for up to 8 consecutive assessment years with no upper monetary cap.
