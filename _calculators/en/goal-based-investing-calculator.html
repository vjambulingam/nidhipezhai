Plan Summary

Formula: Future Goal = Current Cost × (1 + Inflation)Years. The future compounding value of current investments is deducted, and the remaining shortfall is bridged by the required monthly SIP. Taxes, expense ratios, and sequence-of-returns risk are excluded.

Key Assumptions
  • Future goal target costs are adjusted using compounded annual inflation (lifestyle/education inflation may be higher than headline CPI).
  • Monthly SIP investments are compounded monthly at the assumed constant annual rate of return.
  • Mutual fund investments are subject to market volatility; actual annualized returns will vary across market cycles.
