Key assumptions
  • The selected rate is held constant for the entire estimate. Government-notified PPF rates can change.
  • Deposits are assumed to be made by the 5th; interest is estimated monthly on the eligible balance and credited annually.
  • The 80C illustration assumes the old tax regime, available deduction limit and a 30% marginal rate plus 4% cess; actual tax savings depend on eligibility and other deductions. Extensions beyond 15 years require compliance with applicable rules.
  • The projection starts with a full financial year of deposits and excludes withdrawals. Actual maturity depends on the financial year in which the account was opened. Monthly interest shown in the schedule accrues but is credited only at year-end.
