What is Net Worth?

The single most reliable metric to evaluate your true personal financial health.

Basic Financial Formula

₹ Total Assets − 📉 Total Liabilities = ⚖️ Net Worth

Many people evaluate their economic progress by looking at monthly income or newly purchased vehicles. However, income is merely cash flow; only your Net Worth reveals the true cumulative strength of your wealth. If all your debts and loans were cleared today, what remains in your possession is your actual net worth.

Key Rules for Calculating Net Worth

Follow these best practices to ensure an accurate, realistic evaluation of your financial standing.

✓ Do's

  • • Record all debts honestly: Include small hand loans from friends, family, and active credit card balances without omitting anything.
  • • Value fixed assets conservatively: Avoid inflating property values; record only the realistic fair market value you could realize promptly if sold today.
  • • Maintain regular periodic updates: Updating numbers at the start of every financial year (April 1) and quarterly fosters consistent financial discipline.

✕ Don'ts

  • • Do not include depreciating items as assets: Personal vehicles, smartphones, televisions, and electronics lose value rapidly; exclude them from your net worth inventory.
  • • Do not be discouraged by a negative net worth: Early in your career or immediately after purchasing a home via a mortgage, a negative net worth is normal. It turns positive as debt reduces and investments compound.
  • • Do not factor in unearned future income: Expected salary increments, upcoming bonuses, or prospective inheritances should never be counted until actually received.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Common queries regarding net worth tracking and using the Google Sheet template.

1. Can anyone else see the financial details I enter into this Google Sheet?

No, absolutely not. When you click the 'Make a Copy' link, an independent duplicate is generated and saved exclusively within your personal Google Drive account. NidhiPezhai administrators and third parties have zero access to your data or sheet.

2. How often should I update my net worth?

There is no need to check or update every day or week. Once every quarter (3 months) or every 6 months is ideal. Because market fluctuations cause daily portfolio movements, observing the broad multi-year trend provides the clearest picture.

3. What should I do if my net worth is negative?

If your liabilities exceed your assets, your net worth will be negative. There is no reason to panic. Your immediate focus should be systematically eliminating high-interest debt such as credit cards and personal loans. Use our Credit Card Payoff Calculator to structure an accelerated debt payoff plan.

4. How should I value gold and real estate assets?

For physical gold jewellery, calculate based on current prevailing bullion spot rates, discounting making charges and wastage. For real estate or plots, use the government guideline value or 80–90% of realistic market sales value as a safe, conservative estimate.
