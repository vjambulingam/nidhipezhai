Key assumptions
  • This is a needs-based estimate, not a policy recommendation or an assessment of eligibility.
  • Family expenses are modeled as a level annual expense over the chosen period, discounted at an assumed 2% annual real return after inflation. Salary is used only to show the income multiple.
  • Debts and the entered goal amounts are added, then available investments and existing life cover are deducted. Goal amounts are not separately inflated; enter estimates appropriate for the time they are needed. Avoid counting loan payments or goals twice in living expenses.
