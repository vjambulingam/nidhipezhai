Key Assumptions
  • Annual Percentage Rate (APR) is divided by 12 and compounded on a monthly reducing balance basis.
  • Assumes no additional purchases, balance transfers, or cash advances are made during the payoff period.
  • Late payment fees, annual card charges, and 18% GST on finance charges are not included.
  • Paying only the minimum due substantially increases cumulative interest and can trap you in debt for decades.
